**Proposed text:**

>
> We have prepared a masked sample of 100 candidate-years, with 25 cases from each year. The packet includes candidate name, city, state, office, and the election-date context, but excludes our internal identifiers and all known website outcomes. We are requesting a no-cost feasibility sample only and are not authorizing a purchase.
