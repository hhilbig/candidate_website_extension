# Municipal records request batch 1

## Status: approved but paused

Hanno approved the three exact recipients and messages on 2026-08-25. They were
saved as Gmail drafts and were not sent. The project then paused all three
because pre-2010 domains are unlikely to produce strict evidence when the
Wayback Machine lacks election-period captures.

Do not send these drafts unless Hanno explicitly reactivates the pre-2010
sensitivity exercise and gives a new send instruction.

This paused batch covers 77 unresolved candidate-years. It prioritizes custodians with
an official role address and avoids Tennessee agencies whose published policy
limits requests to Tennessee citizens.

## 1. Columbus, Ohio

**To:** `CampaignFinance@columbus.gov`

**Official recipient source:** [City of Columbus Office of Ethics & Campaign
Finance](https://www.columbus.gov/Government/Office-of-Ethics-Campaign-Finance)

**Subject:** Historical Columbus mayoral candidate records, 2003 and 2007

Hello,

I request electronic copies of existing City records for candidates for mayor
of Columbus in the 2003 and 2007 elections. I am looking for candidate or
committee registration records, campaign-treasurer appointments, candidate
contact lists, and election-management exports that contain any of the
following fields: candidate name, office sought, election date, campaign
website, campaign email, committee name or ID, and treasurer.

Please provide the records in their existing native electronic format, such as
CSV, Excel, a database export, or the original electronic form, if that format
is retained. Existing PDFs or scans are also useful if no native file remains.
I am not asking the City to create a new record, research individual candidates,
or compile fields that are not already stored together.

If your office is not the custodian, please route this request to the City Clerk
or other appropriate records custodian if possible. If no responsive records
remain, please state that and identify the earliest election year for which
comparable records are retained.

Best,
Hanno Hilbig
